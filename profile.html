Background & credentials

A career connecting
research and industry.

I work at the intersection of AI strategy, product development and enterprise operations. As Lead Data Scientist at EPAM Systems, I lead AI/ML engagements, help shape opportunities and delivery teams, and work with senior stakeholders on value, adoption and governance. Previously at Reckitt, I translated research into a production application for product shelf-life prediction and developed analytics for manufacturing and supply operations. My work combines hands-on technical depth with cross-functional delivery, mentoring and industry–academic collaboration. I hold a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Hull and am a Distinguished Visiting Fellow at Loughborough University.
