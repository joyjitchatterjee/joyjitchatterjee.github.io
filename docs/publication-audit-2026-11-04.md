## Source corrections and rejected links

- The LDR Operations Ontology entry has a wrong Chatterjee initial and an incomplete/differently ordered author list. Use the 12-author list verified on the publisher PDF.
- The LDR Transparent Deep Learning WindEurope2019 entry incorrectly reuses the NeurIPS2019 NLP paper link. That legacy file also returns404. Use official organiser posterPO074 listing instead.
- The same obsolete PDF for the genuine Natural Language Generation workshop paper is replaced by the current Climate Change AI2019 entry and PDF.
- NLDL2019 conference homepage is obsolete; use the matching author-deposited arXiv record which explicitly states the January2019 presentation.
- Keep NeurIPS/KDD workshop outputs labelled as workshops; keep factories paper as preprint.
- XAI4Wind v2 is dated24Feb2021 and firstversion18Dec2020; do not treat versions as separatepapers.
- The group book-editor mention under another author’s chapter does not establish Joyjit’s authorship. Those chapters were excluded.
