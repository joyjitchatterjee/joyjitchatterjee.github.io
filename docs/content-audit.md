## Deliberate omissions and boundaries

The homepage is a selected presentation, with the full profile and publication
archive carrying detail. Routine attendance and older introductory courses do
not compete with enterprise delivery or invited expert service. Small early
student prizes, training appreciation letters and every routine CPD event were
not added individually; existing historical records were not deleted. No private
contact details, raw CVs, confidential client material or referee details are
published. Client names associated only with pitches are not shown as delivered
engagements. No invented budget authority, P&L ownership or team sizes.

ETH and CV-derived work descriptions use the supplied documents as evidence.
Not every historic migrated entry has independent public corroboration; source
IDs retain that distinction. External pages can change or disappear. The two Good AI article URLs in the
CV currently return 404, so the publication records remain without dead links.
Some publisher/Medium/WEF endpoints block automated requests (403); this does
not establish that their primary-source links are broken.
